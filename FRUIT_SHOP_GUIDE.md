# Fruit Shop - Game Guide 🍎💰

## Overview

Fruit Shop is an interactive money math game where kids drag and drop coins to pay for fruit orders. It teaches money counting, coin recognition, multiplication, and basic arithmetic in a fun shopping context.

## How to Play

### Basic Gameplay

1. **Read the Order**: See what fruit the shopkeeper wants and how much each costs
2. **Calculate the Total**: Multiply quantity × price (e.g., 3 apples at $2 each = $6)
3. **Select Coins**: Drag coins from the bank or tap them to add to your payment tray
4. **Check Your Total**: See if you've paid the right amount
5. **Submit Payment**: Click submit when your total matches the order total
6. **Repeat**: Get your score and move to the next order!

### Controls

#### Desktop
- **Drag & Drop**: Click and drag coins from the bank to the payment tray
- **Click**: Click coins in the bank to add them to the tray
- **Remove**: Click coins in the tray to return them to the bank

#### Mobile/Tablet
- **Tap**: Tap coins in the bank to add them to the tray
- **Remove**: Tap coins in the tray to return them to the bank
- **Drag** (optional): You can also drag coins on touch devices

### Buttons

- **🔄 Reset**: Return all coins from the tray to the bank
- **✓ Submit**: Submit your payment (only works when total is correct)
- **💡 Get Hint**: Show helpful hints (can be disabled in settings)
- **❌ Quit**: Exit to main menu

## Game Modes

### 🎯 Practice Mode
- **No timer** - Take your time to think
- **Unlimited hints** - Get help whenever needed
- **Perfect for learning** - Focus on understanding money math
- **No pressure** - Build confidence at your own pace

### ⏱️ Time Attack Mode
- **60-120 second timer** - Race against the clock
- **Limited hints** (3 per game) - Test your knowledge
- **Score multipliers** - Streak bonuses for consecutive correct answers
- **Competitive** - Try to beat your best score

## Difficulty Levels

### Levels 1-3: Beginner (Ages 4-5)
- **Coins**: $1, $2
- **Totals**: Up to $10
- **Focus**: Counting and basic addition
- **Example**: "Buy 4 apples at $1 each = $4"

### Levels 4-6: Intermediate (Ages 6-7)
- **Coins**: $1, $2, $5
- **Totals**: Up to $30
- **Focus**: Counting by 2s and 5s, simple multiplication
- **Example**: "Buy 5 bananas at $3 each = $15"

### Levels 7-10: Advanced (Ages 8-10)
- **Coins**: $1, $2, $5, $10, $20
- **Totals**: Up to $80
- **Focus**: Multiplication, efficient coin combinations
- **Example**: "Buy 8 cookies at $4 each = $32"

## Hints System

Press the "💡 Get Hint" button to receive progressive hints:

1. **First Hint**: Shows the multiplication formula
   - Example: "💡 Total = 3 × $2"

2. **Second Hint**: Shows repeated addition
   - Example: "💡 $2 + $2 + $2 = $6"

3. **Third Hint**: Suggests an efficient coin combination
   - Example: "💡 Try: 1×$5 + 1×$1"

## Scoring

### Points
- **Base Points**: 10 points per correct order
- **Streak Bonus**: +1 point per streak (max +10)
- **Value Bonus**: Larger orders earn more points

### Streaks
- **5 in a row**: "🔥 Hot Streak!" bonus
- **10 in a row**: "⭐ Superstar!" celebration + big bonus

### Time Attack Penalties
- **Wrong Answer**: -2 points OR streak resets (depending on settings)

## Tips for Parents/Teachers

### Age Recommendations
- **Ages 4-5**: Start with Level 1-2, Practice mode, unlimited hints
- **Ages 6-7**: Levels 3-5, introduce Time Attack gradually
- **Ages 8-10**: Levels 6-10, challenge mode, exact change option

### Learning Objectives
- **Money Recognition**: Identify different coin/bill values
- **Skip Counting**: Count by 2s, 5s, 10s
- **Multiplication**: Understand quantity × price
- **Addition**: Combine different coin values
- **Problem Solving**: Find efficient coin combinations

### Common Challenges
1. **Confusing quantity and price**: Remind them to multiply
2. **Using too many $1 coins**: Encourage larger denominations
3. **Rushing in Time Attack**: Start with Practice mode first

### Encouraging Progress
- Celebrate streaks and personal bests
- Focus on accuracy before speed
- Practice specific levels that match homework
- Use real coins/play money alongside the game

## Troubleshooting

### Coins not responding
- Ensure you're clicking/tapping directly on the coin
- Try refreshing the page if issue persists

### Can't submit even with correct total
- Double-check the math (quantity × price)
- Make sure "Exact Change Required" is off if you're overpaying

### Game running slowly
- Close other browser tabs
- Restart the browser
- Check that your device meets minimum requirements
